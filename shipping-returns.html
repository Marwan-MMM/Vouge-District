Shipping & returns

Delivery options, tracking and our 30-day returns promise — all in plain language, no small print games.

Sample policy — last updated November 2025

Shipping options & costs

We keep delivery simple — two options, both confirmed at checkout before you pay:

  • Standard delivery — $8, arrives in 3–6 business days.
  • Express delivery — $18, arrives in 1–2 business days.
  • Free standard delivery — on all orders over $150, applied automatically at checkout.

Delivery estimates are counted in business days from the moment your order leaves us, not from the moment you place it.

Order processing

Orders are prepared and dispatched within 1–2 business days. You'll receive an email confirmation the moment your order is placed, and a second email when it ships. Orders paid by bank transfer are prepared as soon as the transfer reaches us — cash-on-delivery orders are prepared immediately.

Tracking

Every order ships with tracking. As soon as your parcel is handed to the courier, we email you the tracking details so you can follow it to your door. If tracking hasn't updated within the expected delivery window, contact us with your order number and we'll chase the courier for you.

Returns

You have 30 days from delivery to return any item. To qualify for a refund, items must be:

  • Unworn and unwashed,
  • With all tags still attached,
  • In their original packaging where possible.

To start a return, message us via the contact page with your order number and the item(s) you're sending back — we'll reply with the return instructions and address. Sale items follow the same 30-day window unless the product page marks them as final sale.

Exchanges

Need a different size or colour? We're happy to exchange, subject to stock. The fastest route is to order the new piece now and return the original — your refund is issued as soon as the return reaches us. If you'd like us to hold a size first, contact us before ordering and we'll do our best.

Refund timing

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of your return arriving and passing inspection. Bank transfer refunds go back to the account the payment came from; cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer — we'll confirm the details with you by email.

Damaged items

If something arrives damaged or faulty, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement or a full refund — including any delivery costs — without quibbling. You should never keep a damaged item out of politeness.

Contact

Questions about a delivery, a return or anything in between? The friendliest route is the contact page — we usually reply within two business days. You can also email us directly or use the details in the footer of any page.
