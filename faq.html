Frequently asked questions

The short version of how ordering, delivery and returns work. If your question isn't here, the contact page is one click away.

Orders & payment

How do I place an order?+

Add the pieces you love to your cart, then head to checkout. You'll fill in your delivery details, choose a delivery method and a payment method, and place the order — no account needed. An email with your order number follows straight away.

Which payment methods do you accept?+

Two simple options: cash on delivery — pay the courier in cash when your order arrives — or bank transfer — we email you payment instructions as soon as your order is placed, and it ships once the transfer is received. We never collect card details on this website.

How do I use a promo code?+

Type your code into the promo box in the cart or at checkout and the discount is applied to your subtotal before delivery is calculated. New here? WELCOME10 takes 10% off your first order. One code per order — codes are checked again when the order is placed, so expired or ineligible codes are politely declined.

Shipping

Which delivery options are available?+

Standard delivery is $8 and arrives in 3–6 business days. Express delivery is $18 and arrives in 1–2 business days. The option you choose is confirmed — with its cost — at checkout before you place the order.

When is shipping free?+

Standard delivery is complimentary on orders over $150. It's applied automatically at checkout — no code needed. Express delivery stays at $18 however much you spend, for when you need it tomorrow-ish.

Can I track my order?+

Yes. Once your order is handed to the courier we email you the tracking details. If your parcel hasn't arrived within the expected window, contact us with your order number and we'll chase it for you.

Returns & exchanges

What is your return policy?+

You have 30 days from delivery to return an item, as long as it's unworn, the tags are still attached and — where possible — it's in its original packaging. Sale items follow the same 30-day window unless the product page marks them as final sale.

How do I start a return?+

Send us a message through the contact page with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return. We'll reply with the return instructions and address. Once your return arrives and passes a quick inspection, we refund your original payment method.

Can I exchange something for a different size?+

Yes, subject to stock. The quickest route is to order the new size now and return the original — we refund it as soon as it reaches us. If you'd rather reserve the size first, message us and we'll do our best to hold it for you.

Products & sizing

How do I pick the right size?+

Every product page has a size guide with garment measurements and a fit note — relaxed, true to size, or sized up. If you're between sizes or just unsure, message us before ordering; a two-line conversation now beats a return later.

An item I want is sold out — will it come back?+

Often, yes — our core pieces are restocked as they sell through. Add the item to your wishlist to keep an eye on it, or contact us and we'll tell you whether a restock is planned.

How should I care for my pieces?+

Fabric composition and care instructions are listed on every product page and repeated on the garment label — wash cold, dry flat, and so on. Following them is the single best way to make a piece last; wool and cashmere in particular prefer folding to hanging.

Account & privacy

Do I need an account to order?+

No. Checkout asks only for the details we need to deliver your order, and all updates go to your email. Your cart and wishlist are kept privately in your own browser.

What happens to my personal information?+

We use it to fulfil your orders, answer your messages and — only if you subscribe — send our newsletter. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else. If you'd like your details removed, contact us and we'll take care of it.
